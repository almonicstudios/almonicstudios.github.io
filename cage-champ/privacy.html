The short version

Your progress lives only on your phone. No accounts and no tracking. The game is free and has rewarded ads from Google AdMob, but an ad only plays when you tap a button that says so. Ads are non-personalised. Anonymous gameplay stats are off unless you switch them on.

This policy covers the Cage Champ app, version 1.0 and later, made by Almonic Studios. It says the same thing as the Privacy sheet inside the game (Settings, then Privacy policy), with a bit more detail.

What stays on your phone

Your save

Your fighter's name, nickname, look, stats, cash, Gold Gloves, Training Tokens, record, achievements and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. We never see them.

A small play log

The game keeps a short list of recent game events on your phone, like "fight started" or "drill finished", with the time. It holds nothing personal and nothing you type. It's there so a bug report can say what happened just before something went wrong. On its own it isn't sent anywhere.

Reminders

After your first boss win the game asks once if you want reminders (for example when your camp is full or the weekly fights are up). If you say yes, your phone asks for permission to show notifications. The reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. There's no push server. You can turn them off in Settings.

What leaves your phone

Fonts, art and sound are built into the app. Your save never leaves your phone. Here is everything that does go over the internet, and when:

  • A small settings file. When the game starts it downloads a small settings file (ads-config.json) from almonicstudios.github.io. Nothing about you is sent with it.
  • Ads, when you tap to watch one. See "Ads" below.
  • Anonymous gameplay stats, only if you switch them on. See the next section.
  • Arena fighter codes. When you tap SEND MY FIGHTER, the game makes a code and puts it on your clipboard. The code holds your fighter's name, nickname, flag, look, weight class, record, rank, stats, moves, fighting style and a random fighter id. You decide who to send it to. We never receive it. A code a friend sends you is only read on your phone.
  • Backup export. Settings has an Export / Import tool that copies your whole save as text. Only you can start it, and the game never uploads it. Keep it somewhere private: anyone with the text can load your fighter.
  • Bug reports. Tapping REPORT A PROBLEM copies a short report to your clipboard and opens your mail app with our address filled in. The report holds the game version, the date and time, your screen size, your device and iOS version (as the app sees them), your rank, record and cash, and the last few entries from the play log. You can read it before you send it, edit it, or not send it.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us we get your email address and whatever you wrote. We use it to reply and fix things, and nothing else.

Stats, ads and purchases

Anonymous gameplay stats

They're off by default. The switch is in Settings, called "Anonymous gameplay stats". Nothing is sent unless you switch it on.

If you switch them on, the game sends:

  • counts of what happens in play, like "fight won" or "drill finished", with small details such as the result or the number of stars,
  • error messages from the game itself, so we can fix crashes,
  • the game version and the time,
  • a random 32-character install id made on your phone, so we can tell one install's events apart from another's.

It never sends your fighter's name or nickname, your email, anything you type, or your save. There's no account, so none of it is linked to you. The stats go to our own server (a Cloudflare Worker). It doesn't store your IP address, your country or anything about your device. Each row is kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone, and the stats are not used for advertising. We use them only to see where players get stuck and what breaks.

Switch it off and sending stops straight away, and anything waiting to be sent is cleared. Deleting the app resets the random id.

Ads

Cage Champ is free and pays for itself with rewarded ads from Google AdMob. An ad only plays when you tap a button that says it will, and you get something for it:

  • the Cutman between rounds,
  • double offline earnings,
  • double fight results,
  • a Sponsor break in the Store for +3 Gold Gloves.

There are no banner ads, no ads that pop up between screens and no forced ads. If you never tap those buttons, you never see an ad.

Ads are non-personalised. The game doesn't ask to track you, so there's no App Tracking Transparency prompt and the game doesn't use your phone's advertising identifier (IDFA). Where the law requires it, Google's consent form may show before ads load.

To serve an ad, Google receives basic device and ad-interaction data, such as your device type, IP address and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google handles that under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can review your ad choices any time in Settings, under Ad privacy.

Purchases

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases. Gold Gloves and the Store use in-game currency you earn by playing. If a later update adds an optional purchase, it will go through Apple's own in-app purchase system and this policy will say so first.

Apple

Apple runs the App Store and may share download counts and crash reports with us from people who chose to share them with developers in their iPhone settings. Those reports are anonymous and come under Apple's privacy policy. The game's "rate this app" prompt is Apple's own system prompt.

Your choices

Deleting your data

Reset Save in Settings deletes your save and the play log. Deleting the app removes everything, including the random stats id. Anonymous stats can't be traced back to you, so we can't pick yours out to delete them, but they're deleted automatically after 90 days. Apart from that, the only thing we could hold is an email you sent us. Ask and we'll delete it.

Children

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are non-personalised for everyone. There's no chat and no way to message other players.

Your rights

Under the data protection law where you live (for example the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or similar laws elsewhere) you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. The data controller is Almonic Studios. Write to almonicstudios@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If an update ever changes what the game sends, changes how ads work, or adds purchases or cloud save, this policy changes first, and so does the one in the game.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and use fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services see your IP address when your browser loads the page. We don't use cookies or analytics on these pages.
