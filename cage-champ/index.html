CAGECHAMP
COMING TO IPHONE

Every fight is a read

Cage Champ is a free MMA career game for iPhone. Each exchange you pick Strike, Grapple or Guard while your opponent picks his. Strike beats Grapple, Grapple beats Guard, Guard beats Strike. Climb 24 fighters to the belt, then defend it as your fighter ages.

Free. Plays offline. No account. Ads only when you tap to watch one for a reward.
