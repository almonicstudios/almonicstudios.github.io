In 50 words

Cage Champ is a free MMA career game where every fight is a read. Strike beats Grapple, Grapple beats Guard, Guard beats Strike. Climb 24 fighters to the belt, then defend it as you age. Slip the big shots and beat a rival who studies you. Free, nothing to buy, no forced ads.

In 150 words

Cage Champ is an MMA career game for iPhone built around one idea: a fight should be a read, not a tap. Each exchange you pick a card from your hand (Strike, Grapple or Guard) while your opponent picks his. Strike beats Grapple, Grapple beats Guard, Guard beats Strike. The fight moves from range to the clinch to the ground, gas matters, big moves are telegraphed so you can slip or sprawl them, and a rocked opponent can be finished in slow motion.

Between fights you run a camp: pad drills on a four-lane highway, a speed bag on a real beat, a sprawl drill full of fakes and jump rope. Each one is graded and preps you for the next fight. Climb 24 fighters to the belt, then defend it against tougher challengers while your fighter ages.

It's free, with nothing to buy. The only ads are optional ones you tap to watch for a reward. It plays offline.

Features

  • A card-read MMA fight in one- to two-minute bouts: standing, clinch, top and bottom position, takedowns, the choke and ground and pound
  • Gas that matters: spam strikes and you slow down, hit softer and get hurt easier
  • Telegraphed big moves ("!") that you react to and slip or sprawl, and a FINISH button when he's rocked
  • A knockout moment with slow motion, a stamp and a replay
  • Fight School: the first fights each teach one mechanic, and losses there cost nothing
  • 24 invented opponents across four tiers, boss fights, a title fight, and a rival who reads your habits
  • Title defences after the belt: every challenger is tougher, the purse keeps growing, and your fighter ages. Lose the belt and you're the challenger again
  • Retire a champion as a legacy move to carry Legacy into the next fighter
  • Five camp stations: Heavy Bag with a Power Hook, Pad Drills on a four-lane highway, Speed Bag, Sprawl Drill and Jump Rope. The last four have 3 lives and a one to three star grade that becomes Camp Prep for the next fight
  • A Move Book with moves to learn and upgrade using earned Training Tokens. Every move follows the same upgrade path
  • A 3-fight weekly challenge, a weekly champions' gauntlet with changing rules, a free season track, achievements and a Hall of Fame
  • Arena: swap fighter codes with friends and fight their fighters, played by the AI. No account, no server
  • Bright, chunky cartoon art drawn entirely in code, and all music and sound made in code
  • Wider timing windows, reduce motion, and separate sound and music switches
  • Free, no in-app purchases, plays offline. Ads only when you tap to watch one for a reward

The developer

Cage Champ is made by one developer, working as Almonic Studios. Everything in the game, from the art and the sound to the fighters and the promotion it's set in, is made from scratch and invented.
