Need a hand?

Cage Champ is made by one person, so you'll get a reply from the developer. Most answers are below. If yours isn't, email almonicstudios@gmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

Settings is in the menu: tap ☰ at the top right, then the Settings tile. Your version number is at the bottom of Settings.

Your save

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only, in the app's own storage. There's no account and no cloud save. That keeps things private, but it means deleting the app deletes your career. So make a backup now and then (next question).

How do I back up my save, or move it to a new phone?

Tap ☰ (top right), then Settings, and scroll to Backup save.

  • SAVETap EXPORT, then COPY. Paste the text somewhere safe, like your Notes app or an email to yourself.
  • LOADOn the new phone (or after a reinstall), get into the game, open the same box, paste the text in and tap IMPORT.

Anyone with that text can load your fighter, so keep it to yourself.

I deleted the app and lost my career. Can you get it back?

Sorry, no. Your save never leaves your phone, so there's no copy on our side. If you exported a backup at any point, import that and you're back to where it was saved.

How do I start again from scratch?

Settings, then Reset save at the bottom. It asks you to confirm, and it can't be undone. If you just want a fresh fighter but to keep your progress, win the belt and then Retire your champion in Settings instead: it banks Legacy, and the next fighter starts stronger.

Your career

I won the belt. What now?

Now you defend it. Each challenger is tougher than the last and the purse keeps growing. After a few defences your fighter starts to get old: his stats slip a little after every fight, and training can't keep up for ever. If you lose a defence, you become the challenger again and can fight your way back to the title.

When can I retire?

Once you've won the belt. Retiring is a legacy move: your champion banks Legacy, and every fighter after him trains cheaper and starts stronger. You choose when, so retiring on top is up to you.

How do camp drills help in a fight?

Pad Drills, Speed Bag, Sprawl Drill and Jump Rope are graded one to three stars. Your best grade on each one since your last fight becomes Camp Prep for the next real bout: a longer decision clock from Pads, a wider combo window from the Speed Bag, a wider react window from the Sprawl Drill and more gas at the bell from Jump Rope. Each of those four gives you 3 lives. The Heavy Bag is for cash, with a Power Hook if you hold and let go in the gold.

Playing comfortably

The timing is too fast for me. Can I slow it down?

Yes. Settings, then Wider timing windows. You get longer to pick a card, to react to his big moves and to hit the finish rings. Turn it on or off any time. Nothing is locked or reduced when it's on.

How do I turn the sound or the music off?

Settings has separate switches for Sound and Music, plus a Volume slider.

The shakes and flashes bother me.

Turn on Reduce motion in Settings. It removes the screen shake, flashes, slow motion and the knockout replay, and calms the drill effects. You still see who won and how. If Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone's Accessibility settings, the game follows that too.

Do I need to be online?

No. The whole game works offline, on a plane, anywhere. There's no login. You just can't watch ads while you're offline.

Ads and privacy

Is it really free? Why are there ads?

It's free, and there's nothing to buy in version 1.0. Ads are how a one-person game pays its way. Gold Gloves and Training Tokens are all earned by playing.

Will an ad ever pop up on its own?

No. Every ad is optional. One only plays when you tap a button that says so: the Cutman between rounds, double offline earnings, double fight results, or a Sponsor break in the Store for +3 Gold Gloves. There are no banners and no ads between screens. Skip those buttons and you'll never see an ad.

I watched an ad and didn't get my reward.

Sometimes an ad doesn't finish loading or gets cut short. You aren't charged anything, and the button comes back so you can try again. If no ad is ready, try again a bit later. If it keeps happening, send a report from Settings.

Are the ads personalised?

No. Ads come from Google AdMob and are non-personalised. The game doesn't ask to track you and doesn't use your phone's advertising identifier. Depending on where you live, Google's consent form may show before ads load. You can review your ad choices any time in Settings, under Ad privacy.

Does the game send anything about me?

Your save never leaves your phone. When you watch an ad, Google receives basic device and ad data so it can show it. At start the game downloads a small settings file, and nothing about you is sent with it.

The Arena

Where is the Arena?

It opens when you graduate Fight School, which means beating the Amateur boss in the final exam. After that it's a tile in the ☰ menu (top right).

How do fighter codes work?
  • SENDIn the Arena tap SEND MY FIGHTER. Your fighter's code is copied. Send it to a friend however you like: Messages, WhatsApp, Discord.
  • FIGHTWhen a friend sends you theirs, copy the whole code (it starts with CC1.), paste it into Fight a friend and tap LOAD FIGHTER.

Their fighter is played by the AI, using their stats, moves and habits. It isn't live, so you can fight them any time, even if they're asleep. Your first three Arena wins each day against a fair match pay 2 Gold Gloves each. There are no injuries and no purse in the Arena.

The code says "That code doesn't read".

Part of it is usually missing. Ask your friend to tap SEND MY FIGHTER again and copy the whole thing, from CC1. to the end, with no spaces added. If it still won't load, email us the code and I'll take a look.

Someone's fighter has a rude name.

Names from codes go through a filter, but no filter catches everything. Tap HIDE next to that fighter (or "Hide this fighter" when the code is loaded) and they're gone from your Arena. If you want to tell us about it, email us the name.

Can I just spar myself?

Yes. SPAR MY SHADOW in the Arena puts you against an AI copy of your own fighter. There are also club regulars to fight when you haven't got any codes yet.

Something's wrong

How do I report a bug?

The fastest way is from inside the game: Settings, then REPORT A PROBLEM. It copies your game version and a short diagnostic, then opens your mail app with my address filled in. Paste it in, add a line about what happened, and send.

If the game won't open, email almonicstudios@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, what you were doing, and a screenshot or screen recording if you can.

The game froze or closed in the middle of a fight.

Open it again. The game saves each exchange as it happens, so if you're back within 10 minutes you should land back in the same fight. If you don't, please send a report: that's exactly the kind of bug I want to hear about.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings in the game (Reminders on), then your iPhone's Settings, Notifications, Cage Champ (Allow Notifications on). Reminders only come a few times a week at most.
